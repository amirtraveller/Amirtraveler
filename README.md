## Google Pay QR
Replace `assets/images/qr.png` with your actual square Google Pay QR image. Keep the exact filename `qr.png`. Recommended image: 1:1 square PNG, e.g. 1000x1000 pixels. The included 1x1 PNG is only a placeholder, not a payment QR.
